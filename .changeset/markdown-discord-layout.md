`markdown` lays out a message the way the client does. A list item indented under another one nests, with a circle bullet and then a square one. Blank lines stay empty lines, before and after a list as well as between paragraphs, and `_ _` is an empty line written on purpose. A heading takes the blank lines after it, and list spacing and indentation match the client.
