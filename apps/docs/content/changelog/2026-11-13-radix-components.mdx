The rest of the interactive components now use a headless primitive for behaviour: [Radix UI](https://www.radix-ui.com) in React, [Reka UI](https://reka-ui.com) in Vue. newt/ui keeps the look. The parts and props follow shadcn/ui and shadcn-vue, so some names change.

- `dropdown` is on DropdownMenu. `Dropdown` is the root. Add `DropdownTrigger` and `DropdownContent`. The item parts are now `DropdownItemText`, `DropdownItemLabel`, `DropdownItemDescription` and `DropdownSeparator`. `DropdownGroup`, `DropdownRadioGroup` and `DropdownRadioItem` are new. The `active` prop is gone; the primitive highlights the item.
- `context-menu` is on ContextMenu. Add `ContextMenuTrigger` and `ContextMenuContent`. `ContextMenuDivider` is now `ContextMenuSeparator`. `ContextMenuGroup` and `ContextMenuLabel` are new.
- `drawer` is on Dialog. Add `DrawerTrigger`, `DrawerContent`, `DrawerTitle` and `DrawerDescription`. `side` moves to `DrawerContent`.
- `accordion` gains `AccordionTrigger` and `AccordionContent`. Each item needs a `value`. The root opens one item at a time and can close it. A closed panel is not in the page, so the browser's find does not reach it.
- `toast` gains `ToastProvider`, `ToastViewport` and `ToastAction`. `duration` of `0` keeps a toast open.
- `command-palette` keeps its API. The shell is a Dialog, so focus returns to the trigger on close.
- `checkbox-group`, `radio`, `slider` and `form-fields` use the matching primitive. `slider` takes and emits `number[]`. `radio` arrow keys move the selection.
- `select-menu` is on Select and keeps its `options` prop. `entity-select` positions its list with Popover.
- `avatar`, `divider` and `progress` are on Avatar, Separator and Progress. `avatar` renders a `span`. `divider` takes `orientation`.
- Every portalled part takes `container`, the element it is portalled into.

`scrollbar` and `reaction` keep their own markup in this release.

`newtui add` installs `radix-ui` (React) or `reka-ui` (Vue) next to these items. Existing copies keep working until you add the item again.
