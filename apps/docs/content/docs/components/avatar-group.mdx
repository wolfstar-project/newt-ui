---
title: "Avatar Group"
description: "A row of overlapping avatars with a count for the members not shown."
---

Use it to say "these people, and some more" in little room: who is in a voice channel, who reacted, who is typing. Show three to five avatars and put the rest in the count.

The count is a prop and not derived from the children, because the total usually comes from the server while only the first few members are loaded.

Each avatar is ringed in the colour of the surface behind it, so the overlap reads as a cut-out. The ring defaults to `--newt-bg-surface`; on another background set `--newt-avatar-group-ring` on the group or any ancestor.

## Accessibility

The group is a `role="group"` and needs a name that says what the people have in common: pass `aria-label`, such as "In voice: 5 members". Initials alone do not identify anybody, so give each avatar an accessible name when who they are matters, or list the names in the label. The chip shows "+2" and is read as "2 more".
