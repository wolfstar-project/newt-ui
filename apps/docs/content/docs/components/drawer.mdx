A panel against an edge of the screen, built on [Radix Dialog](https://www.radix-ui.com/primitives/docs/components/dialog) in React and [Reka UI Dialog](https://reka-ui.com/docs/components/dialog) in Vue. The primitive owns the behaviour; newt/ui owns the look.

Use it to show or edit the details of something while the page it came from stays in view: a member's profile from the member list, a filter panel, a settings form. When the content is short and needs a decision, use a [Modal](/docs/components/modal).

## Usage

<Usage name="drawer" />

Put `DrawerTrigger` and `DrawerContent` inside `Drawer`. `DrawerContent` holds a `DrawerHeader` with a `DrawerTitle`, then a `DrawerBody`, then an optional `DrawerFooter`. `DrawerBody` scrolls when the content is taller than the screen. The header and the footer stay in place.

`DrawerTrigger` and `DrawerClose` render a button. Pass `asChild` (`as-child` in Vue) to put the behaviour on your own element, such as a `Button`. `DrawerTitle` and `DrawerDescription` go inside `DrawerHeader`. Every part also accepts the native attributes of the element it renders, and a `className` that is merged rather than replaced.

## Accessibility

The panel carries `role="dialog"` and `aria-modal="true"`, and closes on `Escape` or a press on the dimmed page. `DrawerTitle` is its accessible name, so always render one with real text. `DrawerDescription` is its accessible description. When the drawer opens, focus moves to its first control. While it is open, Tab and Shift+Tab stay inside the panel, and the primitive hides the page behind from screen readers. When it closes, focus goes back to the `DrawerTrigger`. The close button in the header has a visible focus ring and takes its name from `closeLabel`. Use `side="bottom"` on a phone, where a side panel would cover the whole screen.
