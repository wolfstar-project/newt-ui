title: "Calendar"
description: "A month grid for picking one day, with keyboard navigation and optional first and last selectable dates."
---

Use it to pick a single day when seeing the month helps: scheduling an event, choosing when a timeout ends. For a date the reader already knows, such as a birthday, a text field is faster.

Dates go in and out as `YYYY-MM-DD` strings. They sort as text, survive JSON, and carry no time zone, so the day the reader picked is the day you store.

## Examples

### Date range

Set both `min` and `max` to limit the choice to a window. Days outside it are disabled, and the keyboard never lands on them.

### Locale and week start

`locale` translates the month and weekday names. The first day of the week and the labels of the two month buttons are separate props: set `weekStartsOn`, `previousLabel` and `nextLabel` to match.

With no value, the calendar opens on the current month once it is running in the browser; today is the reader's own date, so it is not known during server rendering.

## Accessibility

The month is a table with `role="grid"`, named by the month and year, with the full weekday name on each column heading. One day at a time is a tab stop. The arrow keys move by a day or a week, `Home` and `End` go to the ends of the week, `Page Up` and `Page Down` change month, and moving past the edge of the month turns the page. `Enter` or `Space` chooses the focused day. Today is marked with `aria-current="date"` and an outline, the chosen day with `aria-selected` and a fill, so the two differ in shape as well as colour. Say the chosen date in text near the calendar, as the example does.
