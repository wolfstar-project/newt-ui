A slider is for a value the reader feels their way to, such as a volume or a sensitivity threshold. When the exact number matters more than its position on a scale, use a [Number Input](/docs/components/number-input) instead.

The value lives in a real `<input type="range">` laid over the drawing, so the keyboard, touch dragging and the announced value are the browser's own.

Every other attribute is passed to the range input, so `aria-label`, `name` and `id` go where a label and a form expect them. `className` (React) or `class` (Vue) lands on the wrapper.

## Accessibility

The control is a native range input, so the arrow keys move it by one step, `Page Up` and `Page Down` by a larger one, and `Home` and `End` jump to the ends. Give it a name with `aria-label` or a `<label>` pointing at its `id`: the marks are decoration and are hidden from assistive technology. The grabber shows a 2px `--newt-text-link` outline while the input has keyboard focus.
