A slider is for a value the reader feels their way to, such as a volume or a sensitivity threshold. When the exact number matters more than its position on a scale, use a [Number Input](/docs/components/number-input) instead.

It is built on [Radix Slider](https://www.radix-ui.com/primitives/docs/components/slider) in React and [Reka UI Slider](https://reka-ui.com/docs/components/slider) in Vue. The primitive owns the pointer, the keyboard and the announced value. The value is an array with one number per grabber, so `[40]` draws one grabber and `[20, 80]` draws two.

## Examples

### Named steps

With a small `max` and a mark at every stop, the slider becomes a short scale of named levels. Pass `aria-valuetext` so a screen reader hears the name and not the number.

<ComponentPreview name="slider" variant="steps" />

### Custom range

`min`, `max` and `step` set the scale: this one runs from 8 to 96 in steps of 8. Without `marks` the track is drawn alone, so show the current value beside it.

<ComponentPreview name="slider" variant="range" />

### Disabled

`disabled` dims the whole control and takes it out of the tab order. Say why nearby, because the slider itself cannot.

`Slider` accepts every other prop of the primitive root, such as `inverted`. Other attributes go to the slider root. `className` (React) or `class` (Vue) goes to the wrapper around the marks and the track.

## Accessibility

Each grabber has `role="slider"` and takes keyboard focus. The arrow keys move it by one step. `Page Up`, `Page Down` and `Shift` with an arrow key move it by ten steps. `Home` and `End` go to the ends of the range.

Give the slider a name with `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`. With one grabber, the name and `aria-valuetext` go to that grabber. With two grabbers, the name goes to a `role="group"` around them, and the grabbers are named "Minimum" and "Maximum". The marks are decoration and are hidden from assistive technology. A grabber shows a 2px `--newt-text-link` outline while it has keyboard focus.
