---
title: "Stepper"
description: "Progress through a fixed sequence of steps, horizontal or stacked."
---

Use it when a task has a known number of stages in a fixed order and the reader benefits from seeing how far along they are: creating an app, onboarding a server, a setup wizard. It shows progress and does not navigate; put Back and Next buttons beside it, as the example does.

Three to five steps fit a horizontal stepper. Past that, or in a sidebar, use `orientation="vertical"`.

## Accessibility

The steps are an ordered list, so their count and order are announced. The step in progress carries `aria-current="step"`, and a finished step's title is followed by the hidden word from `completedLabel`. State is never carried by colour alone: a finished step shows a check where its number was, and the current one has a filled outline.
