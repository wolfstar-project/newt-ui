The preview above is an `inline` panel: a side panel that is always on screen. Without `inline`, `ChannelInfo` is a dialog.

## Examples

### Dialog

`ChannelInfo` is a dialog built on [Radix Dialog](https://www.radix-ui.com/primitives/docs/components/dialog) in React and [Reka UI Dialog](https://reka-ui.com/docs/components/dialog) in Vue. It renders in place, with no portal and no overlay, so your layout decides where it sits. It is open for as long as it is mounted.

When it mounts, focus moves to its first control. Tab cycles through the controls of the panel. Escape calls `onClose` (`@close` in Vue). Unmount the panel in answer. Focus then returns to the element that held it before.

Add `modal` when the panel covers the channel, as the example below does. The rest of the page is then out of use. Assistive technology skips it, focus cannot move to it, and a press on it asks to close the panel.

<ComponentPreview name="channel-info" variant="dialog" />

### Decorative actions

A read-only view draws the actions and does nothing with them. Pass `as="span"` to `ChannelInfoAction`. The action keeps its look and loses its pointer, its hover and its place in the tab order. It also takes `aria-hidden`.

## Accessibility

Give the panel a `label`. A dialog reads it when it opens. An `inline` panel with a label is a `region` landmark; without one it is a plain `div`.

Tab cycles through the controls of a dialog, and Escape leaves it. A dialog that is not `modal` leaves the page beside it in use: a press there moves focus out, and the panel stays open. Use `modal` when the panel hides the channel, so focus cannot move to content the user cannot see.

Do not wrap a dialog `ChannelInfo` in a [Drawer](/docs/components/drawer) or a [Modal](/docs/components/modal). That nests one dialog in another. Put an `inline` panel inside them when you want their overlay.

Every icon-only action needs an `aria-label`. Any interactive child needs a visible focus indicator, and meaning must not rely on colour alone.

In the plain HTML and CSS distribution, `channel-info.js` gives a panel marked `data-newt-channel-info` the same behaviour. A `data-newt-channel-info-close` control closes it, and `aria-modal="true"` makes it modal. The panel fires `newt:close` before it leaves the page; cancel the event to close it your own way.
