---
title: "Audit Log"
description: "A list of who changed what and when, with entries that open to show each change."
---

Use it to show a history of administrative actions: role edits, channel changes, bans. Newest first. An entry reads as a sentence, "ada updated the role Moderator", and one with details opens to list them.

The kind of change is drawn as a shape before the entry (a plus, a ring, a minus) so that added, updated and removed do not depend on telling green, amber and red apart.

`AuditLog` is the ordered list and `AuditLogChange` one numbered line under an entry. Wrap the value that changed in `<strong>` to set it apart.

## Accessibility

The log is an ordered list; name it with `aria-label`. An entry with changes is a native `<details>`: its summary is a button with an expanded state, toggled by `Enter` or `Space`. The kind is announced through a hidden word (`kindLabel`) before the sentence. Give `dateTime` alongside `time`, so the moment is unambiguous to assistive technology even when the visible text says "Yesterday".
