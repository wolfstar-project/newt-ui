---
title: "Popover"
description: "A small panel anchored to the button that opens it, closed by Escape or a press outside."
---

Use it for a few controls or a short explanation that belong to one button: notification settings for a channel, a filter, a confirmation that does not deserve a modal. For a list of actions use a [Dropdown Menu](/docs/components/dropdown), and for anything the reader must answer before going on use a [Modal](/docs/components/modal).

It is built on [Radix Popover](https://www.radix-ui.com/primitives/docs/components/popover) in React and [Reka UI Popover](https://reka-ui.com/docs/components/popover) in Vue. The panel opens below its trigger and start-aligned unless `side` and `align` say otherwise, and it flips when it would leave the viewport.

`PopoverTrigger` is a plain button that carries the state and has no look of its own. Give it the classes of the button it should be (`buttonVariants({ variant: "secondary" })` in the example), or use `asChild` (`as-child` in Vue) to put the state on your own button. `PopoverAnchor` places the panel against another element than the trigger.

## Accessibility

The trigger carries `aria-expanded` and, while the panel is open, `aria-controls` pointing at it. The panel is a `role="dialog"` and needs a name: pass `aria-label` to `PopoverContent`, or `aria-labelledby` naming a heading inside it. Focus moves into the panel when it opens and returns to the trigger when it closes. `Escape` closes it, and so does a press anywhere outside. Inside a drawer or a modal, `Escape` closes the popover first and the dialog on the next press.
